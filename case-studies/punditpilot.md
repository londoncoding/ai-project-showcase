# PunditPilot · Passion project

An independent passion project bringing football data, AI match preparation and broadcast tools into one workspace.

![PunditPilot — actual product screenshot](https://mark-north-portfolio.vercel.app/assets/ccb42801db80a5e6.png)

## The problem

Football creators need relevant match information and an organised workspace while preparing and presenting coverage.

## My contribution

I built an application integrating external football data and Claude-generated match briefings, with caching and request-budget controls. The supplied screenshot shows the broadcast workspace, canvas preview, news and show setup controls.

## Design and implementation decisions

- Ground match briefings in external fixture and football statistics data.
- Use caching and request-budget controls around data providers.
- Bring broadcast setup, news and canvas controls into a consistent visual interface.

## Evidence

The screenshot is an unaltered capture of the actual product supplied for my portfolio. The visible match state is a snapshot, not a claim of a current live score.
