# Rehearsal Room

A learning-tool MVP to help trainers shape a message, practise delivery and review recall.

![Rehearsal Room — actual product screenshot](https://mark-north-portfolio.vercel.app/assets/7204a6a28e54558b.png)

## The problem

A trainer needs a practical way to turn knowledge into clear messages and practise explaining them to a particular audience.

## My contribution

I developed and tested the original trainer rehearsal tool. The current MVP includes editable learning packs, typed rehearsal, timing, speaker cues, recall practice and JSON import/export. Optional Claude feedback has been tested through a local server.

## Design and implementation decisions

- Organise practice around an audience, learning outcome and key points.
- Separate deterministic practice checks from AI coaching.
- Label recall as self-rated and retain human judgement over feedback.

## Evidence

This is a screenshot captured directly from the working MVP. A synthetic rehearsal received actual Claude feedback through the local server. This does not establish measured learning outcomes for participants.
