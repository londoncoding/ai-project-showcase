# People Team AI

Practical AI education and capability development for People, HR and Talent teams.

![People Team AI — actual product screenshot](https://mark-north-portfolio.vercel.app/assets/02eb703cbd249154.png)

## The problem

AI training needs to connect with the work people do and develop judgement, rather than stop at tool demonstrations.

## My contribution

I developed a learning portfolio spanning AI foundations, delegation, workflow design, leadership and Champion development, with reusable workshop and facilitator materials. I also researched 201 tasks across nine People roles.

## Design and implementation decisions

- Start with diagnosing the work and deciding where AI belongs.
- Include constraints, verification and human oversight in practical exercises.
- Create reusable facilitator materials and examples that can be adapted to different audiences.

## Evidence

The screenshot shows the actual People Team AI website. My workplace delivery includes training internal AI Champions at BCB Group; the independent portfolio and employer delivery are distinct evidence.
