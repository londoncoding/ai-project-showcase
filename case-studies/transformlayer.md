# TransformLayer

AI use-case and programme management, from an idea through diagnosis, approvals, pilots and results.

![TransformLayer — actual product screenshot](https://mark-north-portfolio.vercel.app/assets/f42fdbc80f1ef9fe.png)

## The problem

An organisation needs visibility over which AI ideas deserve attention, who owns the next decision, and what happened after a pilot.

## My contribution

I developed a platform to organise AI ideas, diagnosis, triage, approvals, pilots and measured results, translating adoption requirements into a practical product.

## Design and implementation decisions

- Connect idea intake, business priorities and ownership in a visible register.
- Give approvals and pilots a defined place in the workflow.
- Keep value estimates distinct from validated outcomes.
